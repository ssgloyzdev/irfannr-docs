Irfan Nur

Also known as 0xLoyz or LoyzDev. Lazy, messy, and somehow still shipping things.

This is my personal docs page. A small corner of the web where I keep track of who I am, what I build, and whatever I'm currently avoiding. No grand mission, just notes from someone who opens 40 tabs and finishes 3 of them.

Background in TJKT / Network Engineering, but I spend most of my time poking at web interfaces, automation scripts, and creative side projects that probably nobody asked for. I'm not organized. I don't pretend to be. My folders are a crime scene and I'm at peace with that.

I am lazy and I'm proud of it. Not in an aesthetic way, just in a "yes, I'll do it later, and I mean it" kind of way.

This page is under construction. Like most of my projects. Some of them have been "almost done" for months.
